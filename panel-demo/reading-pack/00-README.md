# Reading pack

These are the only documents reviewers may cite, together with the plan and samplesheet in `study/`.

## How the pack was prepared

Documents 01 to 10 are digests of public guidance, written in the preparer's own words for this exercise. They are not verbatim copies. Each digest names its source and links to it, and each section says which part of the source it summarises. When you quote a digest, you are quoting the digest, not the original. Anyone relying on a point outside this exercise should check the original source.

Documents 11 and 12 are the University of Northbridge's own policies. The University and its systems are fictional.

Digests were checked against their sources on 29 October 2026.
